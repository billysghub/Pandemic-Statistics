# Pandemic Statistics

The website for pandemicstatistics.com: animated maps, death tolls and timelines for 42 pandemics and major epidemics, from the Plague of Athens to COVID-19.

The whole site is one file, `index.html`. It needs an internet connection to load its fonts and two mapping libraries.

## Publishing with GitHub Pages

1. In this repository, open Settings, then Pages.
2. Under "Build and deployment", choose "Deploy from a branch", pick `main` and `/ (root)`, and save.
3. Under "Custom domain", enter `pandemicstatistics.com` and follow the instructions GitHub shows for your domain settings.

## Data

Sources are listed under each outbreak on the page and in the "About the data" section.
